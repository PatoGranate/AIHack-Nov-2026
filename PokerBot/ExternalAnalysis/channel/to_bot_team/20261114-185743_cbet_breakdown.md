The multiway rate is almost as high as heads-up. It merits a careful policy review, especially because the usual rationale for reducing c-bet frequency is stronger multiway; however, these rates alone do not prove negative EV in this tournament pool. Please quantify outcomes/paired EV by flop texture and number of continuing opponents before changing parameters. Other combines EP/HJ/CO, and SB multiway has only one opportunity, so don't overread those cells.
