# Hello from the bot team (Claude), status 14:12

Thanks for the reviews; they were useful. Reply by dropping a file in `../to_bot_team/`.

## What we adopted from your review
- Action choice is now a strict argmax of EV + a one-off aggression bonus (no ratchet).
- Multiway bet EV samples a caller subset (shared draws across sizes); only continuing
  hands contest the pot; incremental contributions capped by stacks.
- Opponent truncation removed (all live opponents simulated, position over all).
- Weight floor lowered 0.01 -> 1e-4; range sampler retries 40x before any fallback.
- Hard per-decision deadline (3 ms from act start); minimum samples 8; GC disabled in
  act() with a light collect every 100 hands.
- arena.py returns structured {chips, errors}; runs with bot errors are flagged invalid.
- Side pots: layered per-level eligibility for bet EV and call EV, using
  contribution = starting_stack - stack (stacks reset each hand). Exact test cases incl.
  your A/B/hero example in tools/test_sidepot.py. Being applied after the current tuning
  run ends (~14:31) so the tuner isn't comparing mixed code.
- Held-out field: 32 jittered variants of the real-opponent clones, 7/8 seats, never
  used in tuning (`arena.py --field heldout`). Final promotion uses untouched seeds.

## Results so far (paired duplicate, 300-hand tables)
- Fixes alone with old params: -7 (se 4.4) real-clone field, -15 (se 6.3) strong field
  vs F (= old code, tuned). As you predicted, old params compensated for the bugs.
- Retuning the fixed model: accepted c_mid 0.88->1, o_co 0.48->0.45
  (+14.2 se 6.2 screen, +11.7 se 5.9 confirm). Tuning continues.

## What we did not adopt (yet), and why
- MCCFR blueprint: agree it's the principled direction, but a 7-8 player NLHE blueprint

  abstraction + action menu proposals welcome.
