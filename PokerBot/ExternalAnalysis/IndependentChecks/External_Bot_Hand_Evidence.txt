1. THE IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE IS WHO PAYS OFF, NOT JUST HOW LARGE WINNERS BET

The Market Makers wins +48,733 chips / 2,000 supplied hands (+121.83 bb/100).
Examples show large payments from weak callers, not proof of a superior universal
equilibrium strategy:
- external r014 h568: AcQd calls bigRoddy's preflop55. On Ad2dTs, bigRoddy KdJs
  bets90, Market raises TO410, bigRoddy TO1050, Market TO1945 all-in, called.
  Market +2030, bigRoddy -2000. At commitment, Market has top pair; the opponent
  has a gutshot to Broadway, not a private made pair. The complete
  runout gives Market trips. This is a field-specific value-commitment example.
- external r017 h258: Market KcQs calls Lehman's preflop reraise120. On 4s3dKd,
  Lehman AcJd bets145, Market TO685, Lehman TO1765, Market calls1080. On turn7s,
  Lehman bets its final115 and is called. Market +2010, Lehman -2000.
- Counterexample external r014 h362: Market QcTc stacks off on 8sQh6h against
  bigRoddy AhQd; bigRoddy has the better kicker and wins after Ac7c, Market -2000.
  'Call off lighter against donors' still needs hand/board/range discrimination.

2. GHERKERS: REAL RIVER SHOVE DISTRIBUTION IS BROADER THAN THE CURRENT CLONE

Gherkers +41,680 / 2,000 hands (+104.20 bb/100). Reconstructed action inventory:
77 river bets/raises, 60 of which exhaust total 2,000-chip stack (77.9%).
This is a structural sizing pattern, not evidence that all those shoves are good.

Successes versus Lehman:
- r016 h893: 6c6d calls flop127 on Jc2s9h, calls turn254 on 9d. River7c: pot977,
  remaining stack1554, shove1554 (159.1% pot). Lehman Ac3h calls. Gherkers +2085.
- r016 h963: Ac5d calls flop130 on 3s3c7s, calls turn260 on Ah. River8s: pot1000,
  remaining1555, shove1555. Lehman Kd7c calls. Gherkers +2110.
These are consistent with extracting thin value from the observed weak caller,
but neither intentional adaptation nor positive EV against the full range is
proven by these outcomes.

Failures using the same broad line:
- r016 h978: 9s5s calls85 flop on 2cJs5c, calls170 turn6h, riverQh shoves1690
  into640 (264.1% pot), called by Lehman KdKs. Gherkers -2000.
- r016 h242: TdTc set on ThJd6s check-calls100, turn5h check-calls200, riverQh
  shoves1635 into760; Lehman Kc9h has a straight and calls. Gherkers -2000.
Do not blindly imitate Gherkers' river aggression based on its aggregate ranking.

Lehman. Two strong-hand extraction examples:

  turns a set on3s, bets420 and calls a reraise TO1330. River6h: pot3220, stack
 405. Its 405 bet (12.6% pot) is ALL-IN, called by Lehman Kc7s; +2030.

5. RESPONSE-TO-RERAISE HETEROGENEITY

Using opener's FIRST response after at least two preflop raises, at most one
observation per hand, the supplied external tables show:
Lehman 0 folds /111 responses; FisherMen 0/13; bigRoddy 0/26;
gamblerlove 43/46; Phil AIvey 16/20; aydine 12/13;
Durahim 16/65; Gherkers 187/381; Market Makers 14/36; Kojack29/62.
These counts differ from general fold-to-open rates. The responder's hidden

shrunk; cannot conclude never-fold or always-fold from small samples.

DEVELOPMENT IMPLICATIONS (HYPOTHESES TO TEST)

A. Add a structurally distinct defender that can call large river bets with
   ace high/weak pairs after barreling; do not merely jitter ParamBot call_min.
B. Add a weak-pair check-call / broad river-shove adversary and inspect bot
   bluff-catching and opponent-range updating. Retain narrower value-heavy
   river shove opponents too; one observed style is not the entire field.
C. Separate all-in-for-the-last-15%-pot from elective tiny bets and deep
   overbets. Include remaining stack/SPR in size calibration.
D. Against observed donor archetypes, evaluate larger value bets and reduce
   bluff investment; do not turn a few visible losses into global lighter calls.
E. Preserve competitive bot's existing selective value lines; no evidence here
   justifies wholesale imitation of the highest single-table win rate.

All suggestions require offline paired testing with the actual payoff/rules
and untouched confirmation. No hidden cards were inferred or opponent code
claimed recovered. Public sample outcomes do not identify causality.
