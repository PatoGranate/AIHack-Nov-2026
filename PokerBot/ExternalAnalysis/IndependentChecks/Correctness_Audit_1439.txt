HIGH-PRIORITY CORRECTION TO EARLIER REVIEW

The unequal-all-in side-pot examples were not reachable in this tournament.
Every player begins every hand with the same S=2000 chips. Thus an all-in
opponent always has total contribution S, whereas hero's total after any legal
call is at most S. The sp_call test requires an all-in contribution STRICTLY
BELOW hero's total after calling, which cannot hold. Folded players may have
unequal contributions, but their dead money does not alter this inequality.

At a completed earlier street, every live non-all-in player has matched the
same cumulative contribution. On the current street its cap bets[j]+stack[j]
therefore matches hero's cap. An all-in player's current-street cap matches it
as well if the all-in occurred on this street. An all-in on an earlier street
would require every continuing equal-start opponent to reach S too, leaving
no active hero on a later street. Consequently a live opponent with cap below
a legal hero raise is also unreachable. Layered bet-EV slow paths are not
expected to run in this format.

The helper can be useful for unequal-start games, but apparent gains attributed
solely to layering need interpretation: time-budgeted sampling and RNG changes,
other code/parameter changes, and statistical noise may explain the differences.
Do not devote pre-deployment effort to hypothetical unequal-stack cases while
the actual format remains fixed equal reset stacks.

REACHABLE V7 STRATEGIC CONCERN — TRAP OPPORTUNITY

The trap override asks whether ANY live non-all-in opponent has AF >= .45.
It does not require that aggressive opponent still has a turn after hero.
In three-way postflop order A -> hero -> B, A can already have checked and B
can be passive. A's historical AF can nevertheless force hero to check.
This conflates an aggressive opponent being present with an available betting
opportunity to induce that opponent.

check_trap_opportunity.py isolates this branch with an engine-consistent turn
state and fixed simulation outputs. With A's AF=.8, B's AF=.1, A already checked,
and hero estimated equity .9, trap-on returns check while trap-off selects a
raise. Test outputs: trap_opportunity_results.json. The stubbed simulation
isolates the control-flow decision; it does NOT establish an actual EV loss.

Suggested next ablation: require a non-all-in aggressive opponent still to act
behind hero, preferably using bet-when-checked-to evidence. Treat this as a
testable strategy refinement, not an emergency correctness hotfix. Preserve
the frozen v7 promotion comparison before changing candidates mid-evaluation.
