5. STRUCTURAL BIAS IN PARAMBOT FAMILY

ParamBot uses fixed made-hand categories and a discontinuous call threshold:
need = call_min + 1 if to_call/pot > .6. Bet-size variants can win by crossing
this particular rule boundary, not by a transferable poker advantage. Tiny bets

checking back or delayed aggression realistically. Jittering parameters leaves
these shared structures intact.
