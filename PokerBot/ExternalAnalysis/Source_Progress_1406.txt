IMPORTANT PROGRESS

The current source template addresses three high-priority findings:
1. postflop now compares ev + bonus consistently against the best score, removing
   the previous repeated negative-margin ratchet. The bonus itself remains an
   intentional aggression preference requiring validation.
2. The first-four-opponents truncation is gone; position uses all models.
3. Multiway candidate EV samples caller subsets, uses only continuing showdown
   hands, and adds capped incremental contributions. Shared random draws across
   sizes improve comparison consistency. This is materially closer to the
   recommended model than the previous all-opponent called-equity approximation.

These are source-level observations, not proof of deployment or improved bb/100.
The original review's descriptions of these defects remain applicable to its
earlier snapshot and older built bots; they should not be read as unchanged
descriptions of this revised template.

REMAINING ACCOUNTING ISSUE TO TEST

The new multiway EV still applies a single showdown share s2 to the entire pot
plus contributions. When callers have unequal effective stacks, different pot
layers have different eligible winners. Capping total hero exposure with the
largest caller cap does not replace per-layer side-pot accounting.

Example test: hero and caller B can each contribute 500 more, caller A can
contribute only 100, with A's hand stronger than hero and hero stronger than B.
A wins only the main-pot portion it is eligible for; hero can still win the
side pot against B. A single all-caller winner comparison gives hero zero share
of both. Enumerate contribution levels, eligible seats and tied winners for each
pot layer. Include prior-street contributions in eligibility; those cannot be
recovered from current-street bets alone without reconstructing history.

CALL VALUE STILL NEEDS THE SAME ELIGIBILITY CHECK

ev_call remains e*(pot+to_call)-to_call. This is exact only when the estimated
share applies to all money hero can win and future contributions are accounted
for appropriately. Multiway short all-ins can make parts of the pot ineligible
for hero; other players may still act. Test these separately from bet EV.

OTHER OBSERVATIONS

The simulation now checks a hard deadline and reduces the minimum samples to 8.
Measure whole-act time, not just simulation time, and report weighted effective

Their presence is not evidence that the exported or uploaded bot uses them.
