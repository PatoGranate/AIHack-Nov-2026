# Observed opponent style profiles — 14 November 2026

## Coverage and definitions
Profiles are calculated from all opponents' visible actions in WBot's local Saturday real-round files through r038 (38 rounds), covering 43 distinct opponent names. Bot versions for opponents are not embedded in WBot's copy, so these are team-name histories rather than claims about today's deployed version. Kaiserbot and Red additionally have dedicated external archives with version tags; see version trend below.

- VPIP = hands where the player called or raised preflop, divided by hands dealt; blind posts excluded.
- PFR = hands with a preflop raise, divided by hands dealt. PFR/VPIP helps distinguish raising from calling when entering.
- 3-bet% = raises after exactly one preflop raise, divided by observed opportunities facing exactly one raise.
- Fold-to-raise% = folds when acting after any preflop raise, divided by such facing-raise hands. This includes re-raises; it is not a pure fold-to-open/steal rate.
- Postflop aggression share = postflop raises / (postflop raises + calls); checks and folds excluded.
- Style labels are descriptive heuristics, not quality rankings. Samples under 4,000 hands are marked provisional. Table results are not used to assign style.

## Reading the strongest patterns

- **Red v3:** tighter entry than Red v2, while retaining very high postflop raise share; fold-to-preflop-raise stayed near 85%. Do not read this aggregate as a steal rate: first measure fold-to-open by position and sizing, excluding decisions facing a re-raise; then test on a holdout.
- **Kaiserbot v9:** moderate VPIP but much lower PFR/VPIP than Red; more entry calls, moderate 3-betting, and less fold-to-raise. Test value-heavy isolations and avoid assuming a bluff will get the same folds as Red.
- **Gherkers / Attention Is All In:** action histories indicate more raise-oriented and pressure-heavy play than the average tight bots. They are different from each other; use hand-level and position-conditioned reads rather than a blanket “aggressive” tag.
- **Important data limit:** style rates pool positions, stack situations, and round-specific policies. Some teams may change versions during the observation window. Do not treat aggregate rate alone as a profitable exploit; validate by seat, action size, and independent rounds.
